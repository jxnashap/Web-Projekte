Title: Taxes on Meta Ads Placement

URL Source: https://www.facebook.com/business/help/133076073434794

Markdown Content:
Taxes may be applicable to your purchase of Meta ads depending on the country of the 'Sold To' address of your ad account. If your information isn't shown below, information is not available for your country at this time.

**Note:** Meta isn't able to give tax advice. If you have a question about taxes, we suggest that you contact your tax advisors or local tax authorities.

1.   **Your 'Sold To' address is in Ireland:**
Regardless of whether you're purchasing Meta ads for business or personal purposes, if the 'Sold By' entity on the receipt of your Meta ad purchase is Meta Platforms Ireland Limited and the 'Sold To' address we have for you is in Ireland, we add VAT at the applicable local tax rate to the cost of your Meta ad purchase.

Learn how to [find VAT charges on your billing reports](https://www.facebook.com/help/190161391031782).

2.   **Your 'Sold To' address is in the EU or UK and you're purchasing Meta ads for business purposes:**![Image 1](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/mci_ab/public/cms/?ab_b=e&ab_page=CMS&ab_entry=438223955063449&version=1666716831&transcode_extension=webp)

This information applies to advertisers with a business address in Belgium, Denmark, France, Germany, Netherlands, Norway, Spain, Sweden, Poland and the United Kingdom.  
If the 'Sold By' entity on the receipt of your Meta ad purchase is made through our office in your country (for example, your business country is in Belgium and you're issued receipts by our local Meta entity, Facebook Belgium Sprl); the 'Sold To' address we have for you is in the EU or UK but outside of Ireland, and you purchase Meta ads for a business purpose, we add VAT to the cost of your Meta ad purchase.

However, if you're registered for VAT, you'll likely be able to recover it from the tax authorities. Meta isn't able to give tax advice. If you have a question about taxes, we suggest that you contact your tax advisors or local tax authorities.

3.   **Your 'Sold To' address is in the EU or UK but outside of Ireland, the 'Sold By' entity on your receipts is Meta Platforms Ireland LTD, and you're purchasing Meta ads for business purposes:**
If the 'Sold By' entity on the receipt of your Meta ad purchase is Meta Platforms Ireland LTD; the 'Sold To' address we have for you is in the EU or UK but outside of Ireland, and you purchase Meta ads for a business purpose, we don't add VAT to the cost of your Meta ad purchase.

However, you're required to self-assess your VAT charges at the applicable local tax rate. Meta isn't able to provide tax advice. If you have a question about taxes, we suggest that you contact your tax advisors or local tax authorities.

4.   **Your 'Sold To' address is in the EU or UK and you're not buying Meta ads for a business purpose:**
If you're not purchasing Meta ads for a business purpose but rather a private non-business purpose; we apply VAT to the cost of your Meta ad purchase at the applicable local tax rate.

Learn how to [find VAT charges on your Meta ads receipts](https://www.facebook.com/help/190161391031782).
